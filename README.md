### 🌟 The short version

I am a backend engineer at [**Brandefense**](https://brandefense.io), focusing on Python, and I really love this language. Python is the tool I reach for when I want to build, test, or explore a new idea. I also enjoy taking a hard topic and making it easy for someone else to understand. That is why I write articles, give talks, and stay active in open source. I learn a lot from the community, so I try to give something back.

When I am not coding, you can probably find me solving a sudoku ✏️, drinking too much coffee ☕, or making one more cup of tea 🍵.

> *Start to learn, repeat to specialize.*
